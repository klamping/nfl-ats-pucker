Metric definitions & data notes

Your points and picks reflect your personal judgment and are saved locally for this season and week. Leans reflects the historical sample. Deviation is the existing weighted historical research score (0–100), not a statistical standard deviation or win probability.

95% Wilson intervals describe the historical home-cover split, excluding pushes—not a prediction for this game. They assume independent observations; shared teams and seasons may overstate precision. An interval containing 0 pp is compatible with a 50/50 split.

Lineup context is observed at dashboard build time, not reconstructed for the game's kickoff. Official statuses and transactions are separate from projected depth-chart changes; neither is a complete roster-health picture.
